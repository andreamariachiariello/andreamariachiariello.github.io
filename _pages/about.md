I am Andrea Maria Chiariello, a theoretical physicist at the [University of Naples Federico II](https://www.fisica.unina.it/). My research combines statistical physics, computational modelling and biological data to investigate the organization of genomes and the dynamics of complex biological systems.

I work at the Department of Physics “Ettore Pancini” in Naples. My research interests include polymer models of chromosome folding, chromatin reorganization in disease, and mathematical approaches to epidemic dynamics.

My doctoral thesis, [*Polymer Physics and structural organization of chromosomes in mammalian genomes*](https://www.fedoa.unina.it/11538/), was completed at the University of Naples Federico II in 2017.
